    How the course works

    1. You are car A. The panel and the examiner tell you your route: „Nach rechts", „Nach links" or „Geradeaus".
    2. Set your indicator before the junction: Q left, E right, nothing for straight on. A wrong or missing indicator counts as a mistake.
    3. Read the junction: signs, road markings, traffic lights or the officer, and the other cars' indicators. The arrow badge behind each car shows its intention (amber = turning, white = straight).
    4. Decide: drive if you have priority, wait if you don't. Your decision is judged the moment your nose enters the junction.
    5. Clear the junction on the road you indicated. You get „Richtig" or „Falsch" with the rule (StVO paragraph) that applied.

    The course has 12 junction types. Your car starts on each road in turn and does every manoeuvre from it (straight, right, left), so you see every combination. Rounds alternate between "you must wait" and "you go first" wherever the junction allows it. The other cars drive themselves and obey the same rules. Pick any junction type or round from the list and the round buttons; progress is saved in your browser.

    The rules, in order of rank

    A higher rule always beats a lower one: the officer beats the lights, lights beat signs, signs beat rechts vor links.

    1. 1. Police officer (§36)Chest or back facing you = stop. Side facing you = go. Beats lights and signs.
    2. 2. Traffic lights (§37)Beat signs. Green-arrow plate: right turn on red only after a full stop, giving way to green traffic.
    3. 3. Signs (§8)Priority road (306) or priority at next junction (301) beats Yield (205) and Stop (206). Stop means a full standstill at the line.
    4. Road markingsShark teeth (342) and a broken give-way line (341) mean give way, just like the Yield sign. A solid stop line (294) belongs to a STOP sign or traffic light.
    5. Indicators (§9 Abs. 1)Indicators show where a car is going, and that decides who crosses whose path. No indicator means straight on. Indicate early yourself.
    6. 4. Rechts vor links (§8 Abs. 1)With no lights or signs, the car coming from your right goes first, also at T-junctions.
    7. Turning left (§9 Abs. 3)Let oncoming traffic through first. Two cars both turning left from opposite sides may turn at the same time.
    8. Bent priority roadFollowing the bend counts as going straight. Leaving it across oncoming main-road traffic means you wait. Side roads use rechts vor links among themselves.
    9. Blue lights & siren (§38)Everyone makes way, whatever the lights or signs say.
    10. Driveway, lowered curb, dirt track (§10)Leaving one, you give way to everyone on the road.
    11. DeadlockCars on all four sides with no signs: nobody has priority. Drivers agree by hand signal and one waives.
